# Investor Social — Real Estate Investment Operating Network

**Independent product architecture and interactive proof of concept by Crystal Thuy Dong**

[**Explore the interactive prototype**](https://investorsocial.netlify.app/) · [Crystal's portfolio](https://crystalthuydong.netlify.app)

Investor Social is a proposed multi-role platform connecting investors, agent partners, service providers, deals, underwriting and operational follow-through in one workspace.

**Status:** Independently designed and built proposal/prototype. It was **not commissioned, commercially deployed, or implemented for a paying client**. The public site is a simulated demonstration, not a live investment marketplace or financial service.

## The problem

Real estate investing often spans disconnected relationships and tools: investors maintain their own buy boxes, agents manage separate opportunity pipelines, underwriting lives in spreadsheets, and unresolved diligence questions are buried in messages.

The product concept addresses the handoffs between those activities:

- Identify an investor's criteria and readiness.
- Surface relevant opportunities with matching context.
- Review deal economics and supporting evidence.
- Route unanswered questions to appropriate participants.
- Track next actions, introductions and operational progress.

## Explore the prototype

The public demo offers selectable fictional personas and simulated records.

| Area | Product behavior demonstrated |
| --- | --- |
| **Command Center** | Role-specific next actions, deal status, activity and introduction queues |
| **Investor Passport** | Investor preferences, qualification and buy-box context |
| **Opportunities** | Deal cards, matching, gated/blinded preview states |
| **My Deals / Opportunity Ops** | Due-diligence steps and record-level work coordination |
| **Network / Introductions** | Relationships across investors, agents and service partners |
| **Agent Hub / Investor Pipeline** | Agent participation and opportunity handling |
| **Events / Market Pulse** | Network touchpoints and market context |
| **REIDE** | Underwriting scenarios, evidence, risks and follow-up questions |

### REIDE is part of the operating network

REIDE is integrated into the prototype rather than presented as an unrelated calculator. The demonstration connects:

1. An opportunity and its associated investor context.
2. Base and downside scenarios, including illustrative IRR, DSCR and assumptions.
3. Supporting documents, missing evidence and conflicting claims.
4. Questions requiring agent or service-partner follow-up.
5. The next operational decision or action.

This shows how analytical findings can create accountable work across a broader investment workflow.

## Suggested walkthrough

1. Open the **Investor** persona and review their Command Center.
2. Visit **Opportunities** to see fictional matching scores and access states.
3. Open **REIDE**, select a sample deal and inspect its scenarios, explanations, risks and evidence.
4. Review the follow-up questions and the related opportunity.
5. Switch to the **Network Admin** persona to see a different navigation and operational scope.

The persona switcher exists for demonstration only. It is not a production authorization mechanism.

## Public demonstration boundaries

- Sample participants, deal records, financial assumptions, scores and workflows are **illustrative**.
- Values shown in the simulator are not verified property valuations or investment recommendations.
- The demo is not connected to real brokerage or customer systems.
- No proprietary production source, credentials or customer data are included in this repository.
- The public website demonstrates proposed behavior; it is not proof that every modeled workflow is implemented in production.

## Why this project matters

The architectural challenge is not simply collecting investment data. It is connecting **identity, eligibility, opportunity matching, evidence, analysis and ownership of next actions** without losing context as work moves between people.

That same design discipline applies to CRM, workflow automation, decision-support platforms and operational systems across industries.
